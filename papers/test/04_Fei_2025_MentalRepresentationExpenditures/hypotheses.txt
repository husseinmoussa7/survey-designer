H1: Consumers represent common expenditures similarly and reach consensus across their individual taxonomies. (Study 1a)

H2: People likely have similar representations of expenditures over time. (Study 1b)

H3: All else being equal, when consumers deviate from their budget, their taxonomy of expenditures influences their spending and savings decisions even without reminders of the categories to which the expenditures belong. Specifically, when they overspend or underspend on an item, they are likely to adjust more on items that are closer in taxonomic distance to the purchased item.
